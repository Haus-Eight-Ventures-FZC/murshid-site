Deleting your data

Last updated 3 September 2026

There are two ways. The first takes a minute and needs nobody but you. The second is for when you no longer have the app.

1. From inside the app

  • Open Murshid and go to your account.
  • Choose Delete everything.
  • Decide whether to also wipe the app's data from this phone.

This deletes your account and your encrypted backup from our server straight away. There is no holding period and no copy we keep back.

2. By email

If you have uninstalled the app, lost the phone, or signed in through Google, GitHub or Facebook and would rather not reinstall, write to akhil.antony.200@gmail.com from the email address on the account, with the subject "Delete my Murshid data". We delete the account and the backup within 7 days and reply to confirm it is done.

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also start this from Facebook itself: Settings, then Apps and Websites, then Murshid, then Remove. That tells us to delete what we hold for that login, and we do.

What deleting removes

  • Your account, including the email address it was made with.
  • The encrypted backup stored for that account.
  • If you chose it, the app's data on the phone.

Data that only ever lived on your phone is removed by uninstalling the app or by the wipe option above. We never had a copy of it.
