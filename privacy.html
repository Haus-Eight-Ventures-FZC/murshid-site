Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 September 2026

Murshid is a personal companion app made by Haus Eight Ventures FZC ("Haus 8", "we"), based in the United Arab Emirates. This page says, in plain words, what the app keeps, where it keeps it, and what leaves your phone. If anything here stops being true, we change the app or we change this page, and we do not let the two drift apart.

The short version

  • Your notes, moods, memories and everything you tell the app live on your phone.
  • You can use the app without an account. An account exists only so a backup can follow you to a new phone.
  • The backup is encrypted on your phone before it is uploaded. Our server stores it but cannot read it.
  • We do not run ads, we do not sell data, and we do not use analytics trackers.
  • You can delete your account and your backup from inside the app, at any time, and it is gone.

What stays on your phone

Everything you write or record in the app is stored on the device, in the app's own private storage. That includes journal entries, moods, reflections, voice notes, and the app's memory of your past entries. Anything relating to a moment of crisis, such as the crisis support screen and what led to it, is handled on the phone and is never uploaded, backed up or sent anywhere.

If you connect Health Connect, the app reads your steps, heart rate, calories, exercise and hydration from the health record your phone already keeps, only to show them to you. Those readings stay on the phone and are not part of the backup. The one thing the app writes to Health Connect is a glass of water you log yourself, and only when you tap to log it.

If you make an account

You can sign up with an email address and a password, or through Google, GitHub or Facebook. When you use one of those services, we receive your email address from it, and from Google also the name on the account, which we show you once so you can confirm or change it. We do not receive your contacts, your friends, your posts or anything else, and we ask those services for nothing beyond signing you in.

Accounts are hosted with Supabase, which stores your email address, a hashed password if you set one, and the encrypted backup described below. Supabase acts on our instructions and does not use this information for its own purposes.

The backup

When you are signed in, the app keeps a second copy of your data on our server so that a new phone can pick up where the old one left off. Before anything is uploaded, it is encrypted on your phone with a secret that is created on the phone and stays in its secure hardware. What reaches the server is ciphertext. We cannot open it, and neither can anyone who might get hold of our server.

Because we cannot open it, we also cannot recover it for you if you lose both the phone and the recovery code the app shows you once when the account is made. That is the price of the promise, and we would rather tell you here than let you find out later.

What we do not do

  • We do not show advertising, in the app or anywhere else.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone for their own use.
  • We do not include analytics or tracking software in the app.
  • We do not send what you write to any artificial intelligence service. Should that ever change, this page will say so first, in detail, and it will be your choice.

Who can see your data

You, on your phone. The only other party that holds anything is Supabase, as our hosting provider, and what it holds is your email address and an encrypted file it cannot read. Sign-in providers (Google, GitHub, Meta) see only that you signed in to Murshid through them, under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Inside the app, your account settings include a delete option. It removes your account and your encrypted backup from our server, and can also wipe the app's data from the phone. Once done, there is no copy we can restore. If you cannot reach the app, follow the steps on the data deletion page and we will do it for you.

If you use the app without an account, uninstalling it removes everything, because everything was on the phone.

Children

Murshid is for adults. You must be 18 or older to make an account.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and where the change matters we tell you inside the app before it takes effect.
